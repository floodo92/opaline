OPALINE — Vocal Polish
Floodo's Audio Labs · serie free

Una perilla. Esa es toda la idea.

Opaline es un pulidor vocal diseñado para llevar una voz sin procesar
directamente a sonido de disco: brillante, pegajosa y presente, sin que
tengas que pensar en cadenas de diez plugins. Gira SHINE y escucha cómo
la voz se abre, gana aire y se asienta en el frente de la mezcla — igual
que los polishes vocales de los discos de pop que escuchás todos los días.

De abajo hacia arriba, el motor trabaja donde importa: limpia lo que
estorba (rumble, sibilancia, resonancias), estabiliza la dinámica como un
compresor óptico de dos etapas, y luego agrega el brillo "sedoso" con
saturación de cinta y armónicos generados con oversampling — sin la
aspereza digital de los exciters baratos.

¿Y si tu voz no es como la de la demo? Para eso existe MEASURE: apretalo
mientras la voz canta unos segundos y Opaline se calibra a ESA voz — su
tono grave, su "S" particular, su exceso de agudos. El filtro pasa-altos
se adapta al fundamental de la voz real para nunca comerse el cuerpo de
un barítono.

- One-knob vocal polish: SHINE controla toda la cadena con una curva
  musical y monótona
- BODY / AIR: equilibrio entre calidez de la voz y brillo en agudos
- MEASURE (Learn): calibración automática a la voz concreta
- Latencia reportada: el DAW compensa, cero sorpresas en la línea de tiempo
- Meters IN/OUT con bypass de conmutación continua, sin clicks

Gratis. Como debe ser.

OPALINE — Vocal Polish
Floodo's Audio Labs · free series

One knob. That's the whole idea.

Opaline is a vocal polisher designed to take an unprocessed vocal
straight to record-ready sound: bright, glossy and upfront, without
making you think about ten-plugin chains. Turn SHINE and hear the voice
open up, gain air and sit at the front of the mix — the same vocal
polish you hear on the pop records you listen to every day.

From the bottom up, the engine works where it matters: it cleans up what
gets in the way (rumble, sibilance, resonances), stabilizes dynamics like
a two-stage optical compressor, then adds that "silky" brightness with
tape saturation and oversampled harmonic generation — no digital
harshness from cheap exciters.

And what if your voice isn't like the one in the demo? That's what
MEASURE is for: press it while the voice sings for a few seconds and
Opaline calibrates itself to THAT voice — its low register, its
particular "S", its excess of highs. The high-pass filter adapts to the
real voice's fundamental so a baritone never loses body.

- One-knob vocal polish: SHINE drives the whole chain with a musical,
  monotonic curve
- BODY / AIR: balance between vocal warmth and top-end sheen
- MEASURE (Learn): automatic calibration to the specific voice
- Reported latency: the DAW compensates, zero surprises in the timeline
- IN/OUT meters with click-free, continuous bypass switching

Free. As it should be.

OPALINE — notas técnicas

Polisher vocal monoparamétrico (VST3, JUCE nativo, sin WebView). El
control SHINE (0-100) escala toda la cadena; internamente es un "amount"
suavizado que modula thresholds, ratios y mixes por etapa. La topología,
en orden:

1. HPF adaptativo — frecuencia derivada del perfil del Learn:
   clamp(medianPitchHz * 0.5, 40, 150) Hz. Siempre por debajo del
   fundamental detectado; un barítono no pierde cuerpo como con un HPF
   fijo de 85 Hz.

2. De-esser de techo relativo — la "S" nunca supera el cuerpo (regla del
   triángulo espectral: 500-1k son los protagonistas). Techo dinámico
   por muestra: thr = envBody(500-1k) * 10^(ceilDb/20), con
   ceilDb = -(0.35 + 2.2*amount) - 0.12*sibExcessDb (el exceso lo mide
   el Learn). Referencia de cuerpo = banda 500-1k con envolvente lenta
   10/300 ms linked (max L/R), ataque 1 ms — sin look-ahead, sin
   latencia extra. Knee 2 dB, idle-bypass exacto (sin señal ->
   d - banda + banda*1 = d).

3. Compresor óptico en cascada de 2 pases — etapa A "leveler"
   (thr 0.26 + 0.10*(1-amount), ratio 1+1.2*amount, 10/250 ms) y etapa B
   "picos" (thr 0.32+..., ratio 1+1.0*amount, 5/150 ms). Ambas knee 6 dB,
   detectores linked, estados de gain persistentes entre bloques
   (sin zipper).

4. Presence — pico fijo de 4 kHz, +1.5 dB * amount.

5. Exciter armónico — banda >3 kHz (HP preparado a rate sobremuestreado),
   tanh(3.2*x + bias) con bias = 0.10 (asimetría -> armónicos pares,
   re-centrado DC exacto con -tanh(bias)), mezcla 0.55*amount en
   paralelo dentro del dominio sobremuestreado. Oversampling 4x
   (half-band polyphase IIR de JUCE, 2^2: exciter y tape en cascada):
   el 3er armónico de 15 kHz plegaba a 900 Hz a rate base — medido a
   -28 dB, hoy el piso de aliasing en peor caso es -59,9 dB @ 20,1 kHz
   (por encima de audición).

6. Tape glue final (modelo Studer A800 retuneado a voz, sin head bump):
   pre-énfasis RBJ +2 dB @ 3,5k -> tanh(1.6*x)/1.6 normalizado a unity
   -> de-énfasis; compresión por envolvente common-mode (L+R)/2 5/120 ms
   hasta ~3,5 dB (laminado sin caminar el estéreo); DC blocker 1er orden
   @ 20 Hz.

7. Air shelf 12k + paralelo filtrado + soft-clip de salida — knee
   C0-continua: y = x hasta -1.4 dBFS, start + range*tanh(over/range)
   con asíntota 0 dBFS. Material normal intacto.

MEASURE (Learn): estima medianPitchHz (alimenta el HPF), deEssFreq
(centro del de-esser), airTargetDb y sibExcessDb (agudos y exceso
relativos al cuerpo). Todo persiste en getStateInformation (376 bytes,
roundtrip exacto).

Latencia: 10 muestras a 48 kHz (0,21 ms) — exciter + tape en serie —
reportada al host; el bypass interno usa un delay feed-forward del mismo
tamaño, así el toggle wet<->bypass es continuo en el tiempo. Cero allocs
en el hilo de audio. Validado: Gauntlet 16/16 (tortura de 1M cambios de
parámetro, matriz SR/bloque, hunt de clicks, aliasing), pluginval
strictness 10, CPU 0,3 % de un core.

Detalles de implementación que duelen: los biquads son RBJ manuales
porque dsp::IIR de JUCE 8.0.14 genera coeficientes degenerados a gain 0;
el high-shelf del tape debe ser RBJ auténtico ((A+1)±(A-1)cos(w)) o los
polos salen del círculo unitario y explota a NaN (~300 muestras a 3,5
kHz, con |p| = 1.038).

OPALINE — technical notes

Single-parameter vocal polisher (VST3, native JUCE, no WebView). The
SHINE control (0-100) drives the entire chain; internally it is a
smoothed "amount" that modulates thresholds, ratios and per-stage mixes.
Topology, in order:

1. Adaptive HPF — frequency derived from the Learn profile:
   clamp(medianPitchHz * 0.5, 40, 150) Hz. Always below the detected
   fundamental; a baritone never loses body the way it would with a
   fixed 85 Hz high-pass.

2. Relative-ceiling de-esser — the "S" never exceeds the body (spectral
   triangle rule: 500-1k are the protagonists). Per-sample dynamic
   ceiling: thr = envBody(500-1k) * 10^(ceilDb/20), with
   ceilDb = -(0.35 + 2.2*amount) - 0.12*sibExcessDb (the excess is
   measured by the Learn). Body reference = 500-1k band with a slow
   10/300 ms linked envelope (max L/R), 1 ms attack — no look-ahead, no
   extra latency. 2 dB knee, exact idle-bypass (no signal ->
   d - band + band*1 = d).

3. Optical compressor, 2-pass cascade — stage A "leveler" (thr
   0.26 + 0.10*(1-amount), ratio 1+1.2*amount, 10/250 ms) and stage B
   "peaks" (thr 0.32+..., ratio 1+1.0*amount, 5/150 ms). Both 6 dB knee,
   linked detectors, persistent gain states across blocks (no zipper).

4. Presence — fixed 4 kHz bell, +1.5 dB * amount.

5. Harmonic exciter — >3 kHz band (HP prepared at the oversampled
   rate), tanh(3.2*x + bias) with bias = 0.10 (asymmetry -> even
   harmonics, exact DC re-centering with -tanh(bias)), 0.55*amount mix
   in parallel inside the oversampled domain. 4x oversampling (JUCE
   half-band polyphase IIR, 2^2: exciter and tape cascaded): the 3rd
   harmonic of a 15 kHz tone folded to 900 Hz at base rate — measured at
   -28 dB; today the worst-case aliasing floor is -59.9 dB @ 20.1 kHz
   (above the range of hearing).

6. Final tape glue (voice-tuned Studer A800 model, no head bump): RBJ
   pre-emphasis +2 dB @ 3.5k -> tanh(1.6*x)/1.6 normalized to unity ->
   de-emphasis; compression via a common-mode (L+R)/2 envelope, 5/120 ms,
   up to ~3.5 dB of gluing (no stereo image drift); first-order DC
   blocker @ 20 Hz.

7. 12k air shelf + filtered parallel + output soft-clip — C0-continuous
   knee: y = x up to -1.4 dBFS, start + range*tanh(over/range) with a
   0 dBFS asymptote. Normal material untouched.

MEASURE (Learn): estimates medianPitchHz (feeds the HPF), deEssFreq
(de-esser center), airTargetDb and sibExcessDb (highs and sibilance
excess relative to the body). Everything persists via
getStateInformation (376 bytes, exact roundtrip).

Latency: 10 samples at 48 kHz (0.21 ms) — exciter + tape in series —
reported to the host; the internal bypass uses a feed-forward delay of
the same size, so the wet<->bypass toggle is time-continuous. Zero
allocations on the audio thread. Validated: Gauntlet 16/16 (1M-change
parameter torture, SR/block matrix, click hunt, aliasing), pluginval
strictness 10, 0.3% of one CPU core.

Implementation details that hurt: the biquads are hand-rolled RBJ
because JUCE 8.0.14's dsp::IIR produces degenerate coefficients at zero
gain; the tape high-shelf must be genuine RBJ ((A+1)±(A-1)cos(w)) or the
poles leave the unit circle and it blows up to NaN (~300 samples at
3.5 kHz, with |p| = 1.038).
